How to read the S/4HANA RACI

R Responsible – does the work. A Accountable – approves the result and answers for it; exactly one per activity. C Consulted – gives input before the work is done. I Informed – kept up to date afterwards. A/R means the same role is accountable and does the work.

S/4HANA programme roles

RoleWhat they do in the programme
ES – Executive sponsorOwns the investment and the business outcome; chairs the steering committee; makes the final go/no-go call.
SC – Steering committeeSenior business and IT leaders who resolve escalations, approve scope changes and confirm phase gates.
PM – Programme manager / PMOOwns the integrated plan, RAID log, status reporting, budget tracking and the cutover plan.
BPO – Business process ownersSenior business owners per process area (record to report, procure to pay, order to cash...) who decide on design and accept the solution.
SA – Solution architectOwns end-to-end solution and technical integrity: design consistency, integration and extension strategy.
FL – Functional leadsConfigure and document each module (FI, CO, MM, SD...); usually from the implementation partner.
KU – Key usersBusiness experts who join workshops, test, prepare training and support colleagues after go-live.
DL – Data migration leadOwns migration objects, load tooling, mock loads and reconciliation reports.
TL – Technical leadBasis, development and integration build: landscape, custom code, interfaces, performance.
CL – Change and training leadStakeholder engagement, communications, training curriculum and readiness assessment.
TM – Test managerTest strategy, test planning, defect management and test exit reporting.

Rules that make a RACI work

  • One A per activity. Two accountable owners means none. Every row in the template has exactly one.
  • The business owns design and acceptance. Business process owners, not the implementation partner, are accountable for solution design sign-off, data cleansing and UAT. A system integrator can be responsible for the work, but it cannot accept its own deliverable.
  • Data is a business accountability with a technical engine. The data migration lead runs the loads; business process owners sign off on whether the migrated data is right.
  • Keep C lists short. Every C is a meeting invitation. If everyone is consulted, decisions slow down.
  • Name people, not just roles. Once the RACI is agreed in Prepare, add a column with the name behind each role and review it at every phase gate.

Adapting the RACI to your programme

Split the BPO column by process area (record to report, procure to pay, order to cash, plan to produce) if one person cannot cover all of them, and split FL by module when partner and internal consultants share the work. On SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition, part of the technical lead's activities move to SAP, so landscape provisioning becomes a C rather than an R. If your programme includes AI agents, add rows for agent design, autonomy approval and monitoring; the Modern PMO guide covers the governance those rows need.
