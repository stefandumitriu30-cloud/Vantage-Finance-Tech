About Stefan Dumitriu

Founder of Vantage Finance & Tech and ERP Finance Senior Consultant, with more than 11 years on SAP S/4HANA, SAP ECC FI-CO and Oracle Cloud ERP for international groups, previously at Deloitte and PwC.

I work where finance processes meet the ERP: the general ledger and parallel accounting, accounts payable and receivable, treasury and cash pooling, VAT and statutory reporting, and the data that runs through them. My work covers the full cycle, from analysis and solution design to configuration, test plans, transports and go-live.

I founded Vantage Finance & Tech in April 2026 to offer international groups the method of a large consulting firm with the hands-on delivery of a senior consultant who configures the system personally. I am based in Turin and work in Italian, English and Romanian.

Selected projects

Real engagements, described without client names for confidentiality. Projects from 2026 are delivered through Vantage Finance & Tech; earlier ones were delivered while working at Deloitte, PwC and Eurisko.

IFRS 18 income statement by function in SAP S/4HANA

International group, operations in Europe, Asia and Latin America · 2026 · SAP S/4HANA Finance

Challenge: present the income statement in the new IFRS 18 categories (operating, investing, financing) with the new mandatory subtotals.

What we did: a new financial statement version with the IFRS 18 structure, new functional areas and IFRS 18 accounts mapped by functional area, and an income statement by function through G/L drilldown reporting. The solution was presented to the Group Financial Control team and handed over with an operational guide.

Related guide: IFRS 18 in SAP S/4HANA.

Italy's new Consolidated VAT Act in SAP S/4HANA

Same group, Italian companies · 2026 · SAP S/4HANA Finance

Challenge: adapt tax codes, VAT registers and settlement to Legislative Decree 10/2026 before the January 2027 deadline.

What we did: new tax codes and accounts, VAT register variants, VAT settlement and Esterometro mapping, plus a validation that blocks obsolete tax codes from 1 January 2027. A full QAS test plan covered tax codes, registers with double registration of reverse charge and self-invoices, and settlement reconciliation: all tests passed ahead of the deadline.

Related guide: SAP S/4HANA localization in Europe.

Central Finance trading partner cleanup and cash pooling automation

Same group, operating companies in Austria, Italy, Malaysia and Brazil · 2026 · SAP S/4HANA Central Finance

Challenge: correct historical trading partner data and automate trading partner derivation on cash pooling postings, so that intercompany balances reconcile.

What we did: reinstated a dedicated reclassification document type by adjusting the Central Finance trading partner validation, extended the number range to all operating companies, and tested in DEV and QAS with counter-checks on standard document types. For cash pooling, specified and reviewed a BTE-based enhancement with an account-to-trading-partner mapping table, then configured and tested it. Alongside: an MDG cleanup of vendors, customers and assets in three waves.

Related guide: Cash pooling in SAP S/4HANA.

SAP S/4HANA Finance implementation with SAP Fiori

Captive automotive finance bank · Deloitte · SAP S/4HANA FI-CO

Challenge: move a financial services company to SAP S/4HANA Finance with parallel local GAAP and IFRS accounting.

What we did: configured the Universal Journal with leading and non-leading ledgers, document splitting by profit centre and segment, New Asset Accounting with depreciation areas mapped to ledgers, AP/AR and the automatic payment program. Built the SAP Fiori launchpad and finance app catalogue, set up Flexible Workflow for journal entry verification and supplier invoice approval, and supported data migration and user training.

Automated accounts payable in Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP

Credit management company · PwC · Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP

Challenge: remove manual handling of supplier invoices after the move to Oracle Fusion.

What we did: designed and implemented an end-to-end AP automation covering invoice receipt, validation rules, workflow routing and posting to the general ledger; wrote the functional specifications, worked with the technical team on configuration and ran UAT. Also managed hypercare after go-live.

Related guide: Oracle ERP electronic invoicing and SAP S/4HANA DRC.

Oracle Fusion ERP as the financial data hub of a bank

Commercial bank in North Africa, part of a European banking group · PwC · Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP

Challenge: consolidate financial data from many legacy systems into one platform.

What we did: mapped the AS-IS architecture, legacy systems and data flows; designed the TO-BE model with Oracle Fusion ERP as the central consolidation hub, with integration patterns for ingestion and downstream reporting; documented interface requirements and data mapping for the migration.

Related guide: Oracle Fusion ERP for financial services.

Finance reporting design on Oracle Analytics Cloud

Energy and water utility developer, Middle East · PwC · Oracle Fusion ERP, Oracle Analytics Cloud

What we did: AS-IS analysis of financial processes and reporting, requirements from finance stakeholders across several business units, and a TO-BE reporting solution: data model, KPIs, dashboard structure and integration points with Oracle Fusion ERP.

Related guide: Oracle Fusion ERP for utilities.

SAP ECC roll-out to two European legal entities

Commercial vehicle manufacturer, Austria and Switzerland · Eurisko · SAP ECC FI-CO

What we did: rolled out the general ledger on a template approach with parallel and group currency, configured controlling (cost and profit centres, activity types, assessment and distribution cycles), set up intercompany clearing and trading partner determination, and aligned FI-MM and FI-SD account determination.

Related guide: SAP S/4HANA controlling model.
